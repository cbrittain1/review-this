Proposed survey questions

Question 1

What should someone know to decide whether EXPAND: AI would be a good fit for them?

Open response.

How this will be used

Responses will be reviewed for recurring and unexpected themes, then compared with the program’s confirmed purpose and design.

Questions 2 and 3: Live track time

For these questions, live track time refers to the time you spent with others in your track: the final 30 minutes of Weeks 1–3 and the 90-minute track session in Week 5. This part of EXPAND: AI is still evolving, and what we learn from this cohort will help shape its design in future cohorts.

Question 2

Which of the following did you take part in during live track time?

Based on the sessions you attended, select all that apply.

How this will be used

Responses will summarize which live track experiences participants report taking part in. We will compare them with Question 3 to identify where reported experiences and future priorities align or differ.

Question 3

Which two would you prioritize for live track time in future EXPAND: AI cohorts?

Select up to two.

0 of 2 selected

Optional: Tell us more about what you experienced, your selections, or anything else we should consider as we plan live track time for future cohorts.

How this will be used

Responses will help inform where to place greater emphasis during live track time in future cohorts.
